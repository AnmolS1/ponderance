You draw on the page and it reflects each stroke across several rotated axes at once, so a handful of casual lines come out as a symmetric mandala. It's a simple, satisfying toy and I never fleshed it out the way I want to; the version I have started as a quick experiment and stalled there. It's on the someday list. *(Early prototype.)*
