A custom Shopify theme, designed and built from the ground up for a real storefront selling a real drink. The brief I gave myself was modularity and maintainability. Liquid has a way of turning into a single file nobody wants to touch, and I wanted the opposite: sections you can rearrange, conventions that hold up, best practices I figured out as I went and then went back and applied consistently. It's live and it sells things, which is the only review that counts.
